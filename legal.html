Privacy Policy

Have a qualified lawyer review before public launch, especially for GDPR / India DPDP Act compliance.

What we collect

Only what you enter: vitals, Emergency ID details and chat messages. We do not collect ads identifiers or sell data.

Where it lives

In this version, data is stored in your browser on your device. If you enable cloud sync, data is sent over HTTPS to our database and is linked to your account.

How we use it

To show your history and charts, build your Emergency ID, and answer assistant questions. Nothing else.

Sharing

We share data only with services needed to run the app (hosting, database) under confidentiality terms, or when the law requires it. Your Emergency ID is shared only when you print or copy it.

Security

Encryption in transit, least-privilege access and no plaintext passwords. No system is perfectly secure; protect your device.

Your rights

You may view, correct, export or delete your data at any time. Use "Delete all" on the Vitals page, or contact us to delete cloud data.

Children

Not intended for children under 13 without parental consent.

Retention

Data is kept until you delete it or close your account.
